Setting & Story

Beneath a bell-towered skyline and shrouded in heavy fog lies Yharnam, an ancient Gothic city renowned for its miraculous blood-based medical treatments. You arrive as an outsider seeking the legendary Paleblood to cure a terminal affliction, only to be bound to the Hunter’s Dream on the night of the Hunt. The city has collapsed into mad chaos: a terrifying endemic plague has transformed the citizens into vicious beasts, while the Healing Church’s highest ministers hide horrific, cosmic secrets behind locked cathedral doors and blood-slicked alleyways.


What begins as a gothic horror hunt for beastly monsters rapidly devolves into a descent into cosmic madness. As you consume more Insight and venture deeper into hidden nightmares and underground Chalice Dungeons, the true puppets of Yharnam are revealed: the Great Ones, eldritch cosmic entities existing beyond human comprehension. Armed with trick weapons that transform mid-strike, you must navigate a web of tragic scholars, mad executioners, and cosmic abominations to uncover the secret of the nightmare and transcend the fragile limits of humanity.

The Most Known Things From BloodBorne


Lady Maria of the Astral Clocktower: A fast-paced, highly stylish hunter boss who uses blood and fire blade arts inside an iconic clocktower.

Saw Cleaver: The cover-art trick weapon that rapidly folds in and out mid-combo to slice through beasts with serrated edges.

The Rally System: A unique combat system that rewards aggressive play by allowing players to regenerate recently lost health by attacking back quickly.

Insight: A stat measuring your character's cosmic knowledge; higher Insight literally reveals hidden eldritch monsters attached to buildings in Yharnam.

Central Yharnam: The opening Gothic city area, famous for its dense Victorian architecture, fog-filled streets, and rabid mobs carrying torches and pitchforks .
